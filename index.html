Why Fall Prevention Matters

Falls are the leading cause of injury among adults 65 and older — yet most are preventable. Phoenix Rising addresses the root causes directly: poor balance, weak core stability, slow reaction time, and diminished body confidence.

Our method pairs NASM-certified fitness science (the why it works) with taijutsu-based movement principles — drawn from martial arts traditions that emphasize precision, flow, and whole-body coordination — (the how). Every session follows a complete arc: gentle mobility work → taijutsu-informed movement drills → adapted strength and balance training → take-home homework tied to real daily activities like rising from a chair or climbing stairs.

The Phoenix Rising Story

PRAA was a later-in-life brainchild. At 58, after 35+ years directing animation for Disney, Nickelodeon, Cartoon Network, and MTV — and earning a third-degree black belt in ninjutsu somewhere in between (yes, the art of ninja) — Sue made a pivot that finally connected all the dots. Animation demands a deep understanding of action analysis and human anatomy. Taijutsu training is built on balance, stability, and an intimate relationship with gravity. NASM corrective exercise science asks exactly the same questions. It turns out she'd been studying movement her whole life — she just hadn't realized it yet.

PRAA bridges modern corrective exercise science with the discipline and philosophy of the martial arts — helping people move better, feel stronger, and live with more intention. Sue built a 35-year career in a tough industry, and she's channeling everything she learned about storytelling, resilience, and reinvention into building something that actually matters.
